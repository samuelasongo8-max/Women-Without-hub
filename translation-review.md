# Translation review

Please have native speakers review these recurring NGO and safeguarding terms in all supported languages. Their meaning is context-sensitive, so the translations should remain consistent across page copy, navigation, and programme content.

| English phrase | Review point |
|---|---|
| Menstrual dignity | Confirm the translation conveys dignity, access, and participation rather than only menstrual hygiene. |
| Host communities | Confirm this refers respectfully and specifically to communities hosting refugees. |
| Livelihood & Economic Empowerment | Confirm the phrasing is natural for an NGO programme area and consistent across titles and body copy. |
| Safeguarding | Confirm the term carries the NGO meaning of preventing and responding to harm, abuse, and exploitation. |
| Sexual & Reproductive Health and Rights (SRHR) | Confirm terminology is medically accurate, culturally appropriate, and consistent with the acronym. |
| Teenage mothers | Confirm the wording is respectful and non-stigmatizing while preserving the intended age context. |
